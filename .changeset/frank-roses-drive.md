1. Inventory Catalog Management:
   _ A new dedicated page at /dashboard/inventory/catalog allows administrators to manage the list of available inventory items.
   _ Scope management: items can be designated specifically for Rooms, Beds, or Both (Shared).
   _ Pricing support in both TRY and a foreign currency (USD/EUR/GBP).
   _ With drawer component for individual item details. 2. Contextual Inventory Assignments:
   - Inventory management is now built directly into the Locations Page.
   - When viewing a room or a specific bed, a new Inventory section appears in the details view.
   - Smart Filtering: When assigning an item to a bed, the system automatically filters the catalog to show only "Bed" or "Shared" scope items.
   - Manage quantities and add specific notes for each assignment.
