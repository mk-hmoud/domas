Enhanced Student Management features:

- Added bulk selection and bulk actions for students.

- Added student deletion with confirmation modal.
- Updated translations for all new student management actions in English and Turkish.
- Improved `BulkActionsBar` to support dedicated activate/deactivate buttons.
