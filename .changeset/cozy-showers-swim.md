- **Booking Management Enhancements**:
  - **Term-First UI**: Simplified the booking creation flow by hiding specific start/end dates by default, focusing on the selected Semester.
  - **Semester Guidance**: Added standard date ranges to the Semester selection dropdown and an informational alert showing the standard period for the selected term.
  - **Date Adjustments**: Implemented progressive disclosure for stay period adjustments (Early Entry/Late Check-out) with pre-filled standard dates.
  - **Dynamic Warnings**: Added UI alerts when custom dates fall outside the standard semester boundaries.
  - **Functional Editing**: Fully implemented the "Edit" functionality for existing bookings, allowing staff to modify housing and dates via a pre-filled modal.
  - **Inline Adjustments**: Added the ability to modify stay periods directly from the booking details drawer.
