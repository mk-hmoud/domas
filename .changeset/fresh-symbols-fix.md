Added `SharedStudentApplicationsPage` — a staff-facing page for reviewing student self-registration applications. Shows a filterable table (all/pending/approved/rejected) with applicant details, submission date, and status badge. Row click opens a details drawer with all identity fields, a "View Acceptance Letter" button (presigned URL), and Approve/Reject actions for pending applications. Rejection requires a reason before confirming. Exported from `@domas/client-core`.
